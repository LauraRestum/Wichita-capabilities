Grade 2 & Computer Braille.

Berry Amendment+

Compliant.

The Berry Amendment requires DoD to give preference to domestically sourced textiles and related end items. Envision's qualifying production meets the domestic sourcing requirement.

TAA compliant+

Trade Agreements Act.

The Trade Agreements Act restricts federal procurement to U.S.-made or designated-country end products. Envision's federal-channel products satisfy TAA country-of-origin requirements.

Buy American Act+

Compliant.

The Buy American Act sets domestic content thresholds for federal purchases. Envision's U.S. manufacturing satisfies the applicable domestic content tests.
