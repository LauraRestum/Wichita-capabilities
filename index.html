Capabilities in Action.

Wichita-manufactured product moves through federal, state, and commercial channels every day. Below are four currently active programs.

Federal
DLA Prime Vendor Program
Wichita-manufactured product (can liners, hand trucks, VISI-BELT, commercial print) ships through DLA Prime Vendor channels to military installations nationwide. DLA stands for Defense Logistics Agency, the U.S. agency that handles supply for the military.
Active under ABOBSC TLS and H2F contracts. CAGE codes 3BLJ3 and 2A178. Recurring volume.
Distribution
Base Supply Center Network
Wichita-made product moves through 16 Envision-operated Base Supply Centers across 10 states, serving Air Force, Army, Space Force, and Navy.
10 Air Force, 3 Army, 2 Space Force, 2 Navy.
Capability
Federal NSN Catalog Print
Wichita-based commercial print operation with full federal NSN coverage including Braille embossing. Digital, offset, mailing, and finishing. NSN stands for National Stock Number, the federal procurement identifier required for items in the federal supply system.
Turnkey federal print partner. ISO 9001:2015.
State
Kansas State Use Program
The Wichita Campus is the verified Kansas State Use participant. State agencies can procure Wichita-manufactured product through the mandatory-source channel.
Active mandatory source.
Federal Channel

Mandatory source. Prime vendor. Base supply.

Federal buyers have three ways to purchase from Wichita. All three are active. All three are pre-cleared on price and country of origin.

Mandatory Source
AbilityOne
Envision is affiliated with the National Industries for the Blind. AbilityOne is the federal program that designates certain products as mandatory-source from approved nonprofit manufacturers. SKILCRAFT-branded Wichita products in the federal catalog include trash bags, seal-closure bags, industrial wiping towels, VISI-BELTs, and print services.
88% of Envision's combined Direct Labor is performed by people who are blind or have low vision. Substantially exceeds AbilityOne's 75% threshold.
Prime Vendor
DLA Prime Vendor
$198 million in combined contract capacity across two active 5-year contracts. IDIQ stands for Indefinite Delivery, Indefinite Quantity, the federal contract type that lets agencies order from a pre-set ceiling without re-bidding.
ABOBSC TLS: $150M, 5-year. H2F: $48M, 5-year. Both ship Wichita SKUs.
Base Supply
Base Supply Centers
The AbilityOne Base Supply Center network covers 160-plus on-base retail stores across 14 countries. Envision operates 16 stores across 10 states.
Pre-approved AbilityOne pricing. No competitive bid cycle required. GPC and IMPAC cards accepted.
All pricing considered fair and reasonable by DLA Troop Support.
Compliance + Procurement Access

The credentials are the door. We've already opened it.

TAA, Berry, Buy American, ISO 9001:2015, and 93 years of continuous U.S. manufacturing. These are the qualifications federal and military buyers require. Partnering with Envision means the qualifications are already in place. The compliance work is done. The door is open.

ISO 9001:2015
Certificate CERT-0145812. Intertek, ANAB-accredited. Initial cert 26 Jan 2021. Recent decision 18 Feb 2025. Valid until 25 Apr 2028. Scope: Manufacture, Assembly and Fulfillment Services for Government, Military, and Commercial Applications. QA Lab tensile testing on every production run.
Berry Amendment
Compliant. The Berry Amendment requires DoD to give preference to domestically sourced textiles and related end items. Envision's qualifying production meets the domestic sourcing requirement.
TAA compliant
Trade Agreements Act. The Trade Agreements Act restricts federal procurement to U.S.-made or designated-country end products. Envision's federal-channel products satisfy TAA country-of-origin requirements.
Buy American Act
Compliant. The Buy American Act sets domestic content thresholds for federal purchases. Envision's U.S. manufacturing satisfies the applicable domestic content tests.
DCMA Alternate Release Program authorization is held at our Dallas Campus, available as a parent-organization capability. DCMA is the Defense Contract Management Agency. Alternate Release Program speeds up federal acceptance timelines on qualifying shipments.
What This Campus Stands For

The mission, in plain terms.

Envision is a nonprofit social enterprise. Every dollar of margin from Wichita manufacturing funds direct services for people who are blind or have low vision: low-vision rehab, child and family support, research, and adaptive technology.

Buyers do not choose Envision because of the mission. Buyers choose Envision because we deliver. The mission is the reason the work is worth doing, on our side and on theirs.
